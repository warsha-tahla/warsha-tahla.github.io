Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED
30 AUG 2026

Warsha Tahla is personal software run by its owner on a single machine. It is not a hosted service, it has no server, and it has no user accounts. This policy describes what it does with data it reads from a Google Account and who receives that data.

What it accesses, and why

With the owner's consent, it requests these scopes and no others:

ScopeUsed for
auth/driveIndexing and reading the owner's personal book library, and organising its files in one place.
auth/gmail.modifyReading, labelling, sending and trashing the owner's own mail, as an index over daily work.
auth/calendarReading, creating and updating the owner's events, and responding to invitations.

All use is by and for the account holder, on their own machine. The app accesses no one else's account and is not offered to other users.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored in local files under ~/.config with 0600 permissions; the program refuses to read them if the permissions are wider.
  • Data read from Google stays on the local disk. Nothing is uploaded to any server — there is no server.
  • No message body and no attachment is stored in the workshop's indexes; an index keeps what points to a message, and the message itself is opened where it lives.
  • All of it is deleted by deleting the files from the machine.

What does not happen

  • No sharing with any third party, no sale, and no transfer of any kind.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies.
  • No use for training generalised or personalised AI models.
  • No human access to the data other than the machine's owner.

Limited Use

Warsha Tahla's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking stops access immediately; local tokens are removed by deleting their files.

Children

This is a personal working tool. It is not directed at anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it, and the policy stays at this same address.

Contact

Questions about this policy: https://github.com/warsha-tahla/warsha-tahla.github.io/issues
