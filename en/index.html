01 / Origin

How did it start?

From the hand rubbing against the work.

Why «Tahla»? Tahla (طُحْلة) is an old Arabic word for a colour between white and black, like ash. The workshop is a grey zone on purpose: it belongs to no single side of its maker's work, and serves all of it.

A developer's day holds small things that steal large time: a state to look for, a rule to remember, a tool that tells half the truth. Warsha Tahla did not start as an idea on a whiteboard; it started every time its maker said: I should not do this the same way again.

Principles

A good tool
knows its limits.

Every decision in the workshop comes back to three simple ideas that keep it from becoming one more burden.

01

The machine is the source

It works close to its maker's data and tools, where the truth actually is.

02

Measured, not assumed

It asks reality before describing a state, and shows the evidence before the claim.

03

The person decides

It shortens the road and shows the picture; it never takes the judgement from the one who knows the work.
