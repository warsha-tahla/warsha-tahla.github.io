# Changelog — warsha-tahla.github.io

The public site's own history. Its version is the site's, not the workshop's. `_tools/site/build-pages.mjs` in the
workshop reads the newest entry here for the footer's stamp, so the number and the day are written once.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-08

- **The header and footer are the identity's.** Every page wears «ورشة طحلة» from `almukhtar-identity` 3.22.0
  (AM-157): its mark, its motto, the vow and the stamp, drawn by `barMarkup` and `footMarkup`. The page keeps only its
  zones: the links in the header, the language switch, and in the footer the legal links with «صنعها عمر رزق», apart
  from the version stamp.
- **The speaking seal leaves the site.** The «الصنعة لها صوت» section and its two mottos are gone, by Omar's ruling of
  2026-10-08.
- **The work in numbers, and a way to reach Omar.** A section measures the effort (repositories, commits, tasks,
  decisions, campaigns, milestones), and a contact section offers WhatsApp Business and a form sent through Formspree.
- **The site counts its visitors** with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie.
- **The site speaks English** at `/en/`, a mirror of the Arabic page.
- **The site fits a phone**, and says what «طحلة» means.
- **The privacy policy** names the visitor's data: Cloudflare, Formspree and WhatsApp.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-30

- The workshop introduces itself in Arabic: its story, what it does, its principles, and the privacy and usage
  policies. The unit catalogue was dropped the same day.
